BUYING DECISION

Used Premium Car vs a New Car

A used premium car can offer more equipment and comfort, while a new car can offer warranty confidence and predictable costs.

Quick take: Use this shortlist to narrow your search, then inspect the exact car, history and service record before buying.

At a glance

ModelBest forWhy it stands out
Used premiumEquipmentMore features for the purchase budget
New mainstreamWarrantyGreater predictability in early ownership
Nearly newBalanceA middle ground worth comparing

What we considered

Good used-car advice looks beyond the headline price. We prioritise models with a sensible ownership record, affordable upkeep and a role that matches everyday driving.

  • Set a five-year ownership budget, not just a purchase budget.
  • Price insurance and servicing before choosing.
  • Value warranty coverage alongside specification.

Before you buy

Confirm the model’s maintenance history, MOT record, insurance group and specification. A well-maintained example is often a safer purchase than a lower-mileage one with incomplete history.
