10 Most Reliable Used Cars in the UK

A DriveAndTech ranking focused on long-term dependability, maintenance costs, parts availability, owner satisfaction, running costs and value over many years.

The most reliable used car is not necessarily the newest or most expensive. DriveAndTech's approach focuses on what happens after purchase: unexpected failures, maintenance costs, parts availability and the ability to deliver dependable ownership year after year.

Quick answer

Top recommendation: Toyota Corolla. The Corolla leads this ranking for long-term reliability and ownership confidence. The Toyota Yaris is the strongest small-car alternative, while the Honda Jazz is the low-cost ownership pick.

How the ranking works: mechanical reliability, real-world ownership experience, maintenance and servicing costs, parts availability, fuel economy, common faults, owner satisfaction and long-term value are considered together.

1. Toyota Corolla

The Corolla is the gold-standard recommendation. Properly maintained examples can cover very high mileages, and the model combines durable petrol and hybrid engines, straightforward servicing, widely available parts and strong owner satisfaction.

Ownership snapshot

  • Reliability: five stars.
  • Hybrid fuel economy: up to 65 mpg in the published guide.
  • Typical annual servicing: about £180–£350.
  • Typical purchase range in the guide: £5,000–£15,000 depending on age, mileage and specification.

What to check

Prioritise full service history, MOT history, accident-repair evidence, tyres, brakes, warning lights and hybrid battery health where applicable. Service history matters more than mileage alone.

2. Toyota Yaris

The Yaris brings Toyota reliability into a smaller, cheaper package. It is especially suited to first-time drivers, commuters and budget-conscious motorists. The guide highlights low insurance, affordable servicing and strong fuel economy, with a typical purchase range of £3,500–£10,000 depending on the example.

3. Honda Jazz

The Jazz is a strong low-cost ownership choice. Its dependable petrol engines, clever interior packaging, visibility and straightforward engineering make it an excellent option for buyers who value peace of mind over performance or prestige.

4–10: Other dependable choices

The Toyota Auris offers family practicality and Toyota dependability. The Lexus CT 200h combines premium comfort with Toyota hybrid engineering. The Honda Civic is the practical everyday option. The Mazda 3 petrol suits drivers who want more engagement. The Hyundai i30 and Kia Ceed offer strong value for family ownership, while the Skoda Octavia is particularly useful for families and motorway drivers.

What does reliable actually mean?

Reliability is more than never breaking down. A dependable used car should start consistently, cover high mileage with routine maintenance, have durable major components, be affordable to service and repair, have available parts, avoid recurring costly faults and deliver predictable ownership year after year.

The most important used-car lesson

Condition and maintenance history can matter more than the odometer. A well-maintained higher-mileage vehicle with complete records can be a safer purchase than a lower-mileage example that has been neglected.

DriveAndTech verdict

If reliability is the deciding factor, the Toyota Corolla is DriveAndTech's first choice. The Yaris and Jazz are excellent alternatives when a smaller or lower-cost car makes more sense. Whichever model you choose, buy the individual car's condition and history, not just its badge.
