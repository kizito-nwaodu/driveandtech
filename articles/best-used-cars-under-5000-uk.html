10 Best Used Cars Under £5,000 UK

A practical budget-car comparison focused on reliability, running costs, insurance, fuel economy, practicality, parts availability and value.

A £5,000 budget can still buy a dependable used car, but price alone is a poor way to judge value. The individual vehicle's maintenance history, MOT record, condition and likely running costs matter just as much as the model.

Quick answer

Best overall: Toyota Yaris. DriveAndTech places the Yaris first for its reliability, low running costs, easy maintenance and generally affordable insurance. The Honda Jazz is the reliability alternative, the Ford Fiesta is the first-car choice, and the Skoda Octavia is the family pick.

1. Toyota Yaris

The Yaris is the editor's choice because it provides the confidence most buyers want from a £5,000 car. Well-maintained examples can exceed 150,000 miles, while servicing, insurance and everyday running costs can remain manageable.

Best for

  • First-time drivers.
  • Daily commuters.
  • Low insurance costs.
  • Budget-conscious motorists.

Buying advice

Do not pay a premium simply for low mileage. A higher-mileage Yaris with complete service records, regular oil changes and a clean MOT history can be the better purchase.

2. Honda Jazz

The Jazz is the low-running-cost and reliability alternative. Its clever interior and Magic Seats make it unusually practical for a small hatchback. Inspect service history, clutch operation, suspension, brakes, MOT advisories and signs of accident repairs.

3. Ford Fiesta

The Fiesta is the first-car choice and the driver's option. It is enjoyable to drive, has good parts availability and is familiar to independent garages. Maintenance history is especially important, including timing-belt maintenance where applicable.

4. Skoda Octavia

The Octavia is the family choice. It offers more space than the small hatchbacks at the top of the ranking and can make sense for families that need a large boot and motorway ability.

5. Volkswagen Golf

The Golf offers comfort, refinement and a strong motorway experience. It can be a very good buy, but condition and maintenance matter. Automatic examples deserve particular attention to gearbox servicing and operation.

6–10: Other strong choices

The Hyundai i30 is the value pick, the Kia Ceed is a strong long-term family choice, the Mazda 3 is for drivers who enjoy driving, the Toyota Auris offers economy and Toyota dependability, and the Vauxhall Astra remains a practical budget option.

How DriveAndTech chose them

The guide considers reliability, running costs, fuel economy, insurance, parts availability, owner satisfaction and value for money. It also considers MOT records, common faults and long-term ownership expenses.

DriveAndTech expert tip: do not become fixated on mileage. Maintenance tells you more about a used car than the odometer alone.

Before you buy

Inspect the service history, MOT history, tyres, brakes, suspension, clutch, gearbox, air conditioning, electrical systems and evidence of accident repairs. Test-drive the car from cold if possible and compare the physical condition with the seller's description.

DriveAndTech verdict

For most buyers with a £5,000 budget, the Toyota Yaris is the safest all-round recommendation. The Honda Jazz is a close alternative for reliability and practicality, while the Fiesta, Octavia, Golf and i30 cover different needs. The best purchase is the best-maintained example, not necessarily the newest or lowest-mileage one.
