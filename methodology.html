EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY

How we make recommendations.

Every DriveAndTech car guide uses the same ownership-focused lens. A car must make sense beyond the test drive and the advertising headline.

01

Reliability

We consider known ownership patterns, durability and the likelihood of expensive surprises.

02

Running costs

We look at fuel, servicing, insurance, parts and costs after purchase.

03

Safety

Safety matters alongside the practical realities of family, motorway and urban driving.

04

Practicality

Cabin room, boot space, comfort and ease of daily use all influence a recommendation.

05

Value

We assess whether the overall package makes financial sense for the intended buyer.

06

Long-term fit

We aim to explain who a car suits, and when a different choice may be wiser.
